How we work

A straightforward path to accreditation

Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence, adapted to your lab's starting point.

1

Gap assessment

We review your current documentation, processes and infrastructure against the target standard.

2

Roadmap & documentation

A scoped plan and the quality manuals, SOPs and records the standard requires.

3

Training & implementation

Hands-on training for your team as new procedures go live on the floor.

4

Internal audit & mock assessment

A dry run that surfaces gaps before the real assessors do.

5

Assessment support & surveillance

We stay with you through the formal assessment and ongoing renewal cycles.
